How to play

Drag the pieces onto the 8×8 board. Fill a full row or column to clear it. Clear several lines at once, or several moves in a row, to earn combo points. The game ends when none of the pieces fit.

Frequently asked questions

Is my progress saved? Yes. Your game and best score are saved on your device automatically. Deleting the app deletes them.

Why is there no sound? Check that your phone isn't in silent mode and that sound is on in the game's settings.

How can I change my ad privacy choices? In the EU and UK, open the game's settings and tap “Privacy Options”. On iOS you can also go to Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Nasıl oynanır?

Parçaları 8×8 tahtaya sürükle. Bir satırı ya da sütunu tamamen doldurunca temizlenir. Aynı anda birden fazla satır temizleyerek ya da art arda temizleyerek combo puanı kazan. Hiçbir parça sığmayınca oyun biter.
